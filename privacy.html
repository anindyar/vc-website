Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Data We Collect

Account email and authentication credentials. Infrastructure connection metadata (hostnames, port numbers). Operational audit logs including AI reasoning for actions taken.

2. How We Use Data

To provide the service, process natural language queries, execute approved operations, and maintain audit trails. We do not sell user data.

3. Data Storage

Data is stored encrypted at rest. Infrastructure credentials are never logged or exposed in queries. Sensitive fields are obfuscated in transit and at rest.

4. Data Retention

Audit logs are retained for 90 days on Free plans, 1 year on Pro, and configurable on Enterprise. Account data is deleted within 30 days of account closure.

5. Third Parties

We use standard cloud infrastructure providers. We do not share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

6. Your Rights

You may request data export or deletion at any time. Contact hello@vibecomputing.ai.

7. Security

All connections use TLS. Infrastructure agents use zero-trust mutual TLS. See our Terms of Service for usage guidelines.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be notified by email.
