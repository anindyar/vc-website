Manage Backups with AI

Backups fail silently — you find out on restore day. Describe what you want protected — the AI monitors health, verifies restores, and audits retention, on every stack.

Everyone has a backup strategy until they need a restore. Jobs that report success but skip verification. Retention policies that quietly expired the one copy you needed. Restore tests that live on a to-do list from two quarters ago. A sprawl of scripts, cron jobs, and vendor consoles that each see one slice of the truth. Whether you run Veeam, Commvault, Restic, Bacula, Borg, or Synology; target S3, Backblaze, Azure Blob, tape, or a NAS in a closet — VibeComputing handles the full spectrum of backup operations: health monitoring, restore verification, retention audits, capacity forecasting, and RPO/RTO reality checks. Stop trusting green checkmarks. Just ask what's actually recoverable.

The agent connects in seconds and immediately maps the landscape — job histories and failure patterns, verification status versus claims, retention windows versus policy, repository capacity trends, and immutability posture. "Which backups haven't been restore-tested this quarter?" "When did this job last actually verify a restore, not just write a file?" "Do we have an immutable, off-site copy of the finance database?" "What's our real RPO if the primary dies right now?" The AI correlates job logs, repository state, and policy documents into a single recovery picture — and tells you where the gaps are before an incident does.

For infrastructure and platform teams, VibeComputing fits existing workflows without ceremony. The zero-trust outbound-only agent model works inside locked-down networks — no inbound ports, no backup repositories exposed to the internet. Read-only by default: the AI analyzes backup metadata, never copies backup data, and every retention change, test-restore, or capacity expansion is shown as the exact command before it runs. Sensitive identifiers are obfuscated before they reach any model. Humans approve every mutation. Combined with BYOK for strict control over your AI provider and air-gapped deployment for regulated environments, it's the safest way to manage backups with AI.

Example:

$ verify backup health for prod-databases

→[OBFUSCATING] Masking hostnames, paths, and identifiers...

→ 41 jobs: 38 report "successful" — only 9 passed restore-verification this quarter

→ prod-db-02: last verified 87 days ago; snapshot chain has a 6-day gap (Jan 12–18)

→ Retention drift: finance-archive keeps 30d, policy requires 365d — earliest copy already expired

→ Capacity: repository pool at 91%, trend hits 100% in 23 days

Verdict: 3 silent risks. Proposed: schedule isolated restore-test for prod-db-02, correct finance-archive retention (exact commands attached), expand pool +4TB. Awaiting your approval.
