VMWare environments are powerful but complex. vCenter, ESXi hosts, vSwitches, resource pools, datastores — navigating the full stack typically requires deep expertise and constant context-switching. VibeComputing brings AI to VMWare management, letting you handle day-to-day operations through natural language. "Migrate the database VM to the other host," "show me which VMs are over-provisioned on CPU," or "take a snapshot of all VMs in the finance cluster before the patch window" — all executed through a single conversational interface.

The platform integrates with your existing VMWare deployment without requiring agents inside every guest VM. Connect to vCenter or ESXi hosts directly, and VibeComputing's AI maps your virtual infrastructure, understands relationships between VMs, hosts, and datastores, and can execute operations across the entire stack. Data obfuscation ensures that sensitive hostnames, internal IPs, and vCenter credentials are tokenized before any AI processing occurs, so you maintain full compliance with internal security policies.

For enterprises running hybrid VMWare estates — some on-prem, some in cloud-hosted VMWare like VMware Cloud on AWS — VibeComputing provides a unified view. Ask questions that span environments: "Compare resource utilization between our on-prem VMWare cluster and our cloud VMs" or "Find any VM running anywhere that hasn't been patched in 30 days." The AI handles the orchestration across providers and presents a consolidated answer.

Example:

$ migrate the database VM to the other ESXi host

→[OBFUSCATING] vcenter-prod → [VCENTER_1]

→[PLANNING] vMotion: db-server-01 → esxi-02

→[EXECUTING] Migration in progress...

vMotion complete. db-server-01 now running on esxi-02.
