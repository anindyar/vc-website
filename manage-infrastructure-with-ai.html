VibeComputing redefines how teams manage infrastructure with AI at its core. Instead of switching between five different dashboards, you describe what you want in plain English. The AI translates your intent into the right commands, executes them across your fleet, and reports back with a clear summary. Whether you're running a handful of cloud VMs or hundreds of bare-metal servers across multiple data centers, the experience is the same: type it, get it done.

What makes this different from traditional automation tools is context. The AI understands your infrastructure topology, knows which servers are production vs. staging, remembers recent changes, and can reason about dependencies. Ask it to "roll out the new config to staging first, then production if health checks pass" and it will sequence the rollout appropriately. Security is built in — every IP address, hostname, and secret is automatically obfuscated before reaching the LLM, then restored on execution.

Built by engineers with over 15 years of cybersecurity experience, VibeComputing is designed for production environments. Zero-trust, outbound-only agents mean no inbound ports are ever opened on your servers. Credentials never leave your network. And for organizations with the strictest requirements — government, defense, regulated industries — an air-gapped appliance runs the entire AI stack on-premises with zero external connectivity.
