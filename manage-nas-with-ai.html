The quiet-box problem: the NAS sits in a corner for years, the lights are green, everyone backs up to it, nobody logs in. Until the day the RAID is degraded, three disks have climbing pending sectors, and the last scrub that actually completed was two months ago. Whether you run Synology DSM, QNAP QTS, TrueNAS, Unraid, or plain ZFS on Linux — VibeComputing handles the full spectrum of storage operations: SMART attribute trending, RAID and pool health, scrub and parity verification, snapshot and replication audits, capacity forecasting, and share permission reviews. Stop trusting the green light. Start knowing what the disks know.

The agent connects in seconds and maps the landscape — which disks have reallocated or pending sectors trending the wrong way, which scrub last completed cleanly versus aborted halfway, which volume hits the 80% performance cliff and when, which snapshots are quietly eating the pool, which shares have drifted world-readable. "Is drive 2 actually failing or just old?" "When do we run out of space at current growth?" "Did last month's scrub find anything?" "What grew the pool 200GB this week?" The AI correlates SMART trends, scrub results, and capacity growth into a single timeline — and separates the disk you should replace next month from the one you should replace tonight.

For infrastructure and homelab teams alike, VibeComputing fits existing setups without ceremony. The zero-trust outbound-only agent model works inside locked-down networks — no inbound ports, no management interfaces exposed to the internet. Read-only by default: the AI analyzes SMART data, pool status, and dataset layouts, and every drive replacement step, snapshot policy change, or share permission fix is shown as the exact command before it runs. Sensitive identifiers are obfuscated before they reach any model. Humans approve every mutation. Combined with BYOK for strict control over your AI provider and air-gapped deployment for regulated environments, it's the safest way to manage storage with AI.
